StudyLapse Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 August 2026

StudyLapse works entirely without an account, and collects nothing unless you choose to sign in. There are no analytics and no tracking. Your time-lapse videos never leave your device under any circumstances.

What the app stores

When you record a study session, StudyLapse saves the following in the app's private storage area on your iPhone:

  • Session duration, subject name, and start date
  • The time-lapse video generated from that session
  • A note of any interruptions (for example, the screen locking) so the app can explain gaps in the video

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share a video yourself using the iOS share sheet. If you do, the video goes wherever you send it, and this policy no longer governs it.

Camera

StudyLapse requests camera access to build a time lapse. While a session is running, the app captures a single still frame every few seconds and appends it directly to a video file on your device. It does not record audio, does not record continuous video, and cannot use the camera while the app is in the background — iOS does not permit this. The green recording indicator in the iOS status bar is shown whenever the camera is active.

You may decline camera access. If you do, StudyLapse still works as a study timer; it simply produces no video.

If you sign in (optional)

Signing in is never required. Every feature of StudyLapse — the timer, the time lapses, and your full history — works with no account at all. An account exists only so your session history can be backed up if you lose your phone.

If you do sign in, we store:

  • Your email address, used solely to identify your account and send you a sign-in code. It is never used for marketing.
  • Session statistics you choose to back up: subject, start time, duration, frame count, file size, and the number of interruptions.

Your videos are never uploaded, whether you are signed in or not. Backup covers statistics only.

This data is stored on Supabase, our hosting provider, and protected by row-level security so that no other user can read it. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising, and it is never used to track you across other apps or websites.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app: Account → Delete account. This permanently erases your account and every session backed up from it. It takes effect immediately and cannot be undone. Sessions stored on your phone are not affected, and you can carry on using StudyLapse without an account.

What the app does not do

  • No account required to use any feature
  • No video ever leaves your device
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no data shared with data brokers
  • No access to your Photos library

Deleting your data

Deleting a session inside the app removes both the record and its video file. Deleting the app removes everything it ever stored on your device. If you created an account, use Account → Delete account to erase the server-side copy as well; deleting the app alone does not do that.

Children

StudyLapse is safe for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version adds features that send more data — for example, sharing a time lapse with friends — this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the app will ask for your consent before sending anything.
